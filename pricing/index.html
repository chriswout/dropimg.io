Looking for Drops pricing?

Drops are free for temporary image sharing. Drops Pro adds longer expiry and password protection for €2.99/month. Web Assets subscriptions do not change Drop expiry or password features.

Pricing questions

Can I buy Developer or Pro today?

Yes. Choose Developer or Pro on this page. Checkout is PayPal. Free does not require a card.

Are the plans metered?

Yes. Each plan has project, storage, and delivery limits. There is no per-call MCP meter and no extra egress line item.

What about Drops?

Temporary Drops stay free at the current site limits. Drops Pro is still €2.99/month for longer Drop links and passwords. Web Assets storage is billed on the table above.
