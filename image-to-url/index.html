    From file to link

    Use the uploader above. When the upload finishes the share URL is ready to copy, and the expiry you picked is shown next to it so you always know how long it will work.

    What you get

    • A short public page for the image.
    • A direct image URL under /i/… for embedding where allowed.
    • Automatic deletion when the lifetime you chose runs out.
    • PNG, JPEG, WebP, and GIF up to 10 MB, with metadata stripped when supported.

    Upload an image, get a URL — without an account

    Anonymous uploading is the default, not a trial. Signing in is optional: a free account keeps your last 10 active links in one place, and Pro adds links up to 90 days, 50 MB uploads, password protection, and browser extension and ShareX uploads.

    Not a CDN or a gallery

    dropimg.io converts an image to a link for temporary sharing. There is no public gallery and no permanent storage tier — if you need the file online indefinitely, use a host built for that.

    Image to URL questions

    Do I need an account to turn an image into a URL?

    No. Uploading and sharing works without signing up. An optional free account keeps your last 10 active links together, and Pro adds longer links, larger uploads, and passwords.

    How long will the image URL work?

    As long as you chose before uploading: 1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days. Seven days is the default. Pro can keep a link online for up to 90 days from the original upload.

    Is this permanent image hosting?

    No. Every dropimg.io URL expires and the file is deleted afterwards. It is built for temporary sharing, not permanent storage.

    What files can I convert to a URL?

    PNG, JPEG, WebP, and GIF up to 10 MB on the free tier, or up to 50 MB with Pro. SVG is not accepted.
