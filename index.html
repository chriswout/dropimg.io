    FAQ

    How long do images stay online?

    As long as you choose: 1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days, with 7 days selected by default. Pro links can last up to 90 days.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Upload and share without signing up. An account is optional — free accounts keep your last 10 active links together, and Pro adds longer links, larger uploads, and passwords.

    Can I delete an image early?

    Yes. After upload you get a private delete control to remove it before expiry.
