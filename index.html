    Frequently asked questions

    What is dropimg.io?

    dropimg.io is a temporary image-sharing service that lets users paste, drag, or upload an image and instantly create a shareable link that expires after 24 hours.

    How long do images stay online?

    Images uploaded to dropimg.io expire automatically 24 hours after upload.

    Do I need an account to use dropimg.io?

    No. dropimg.io does not require an account to upload or share an image.

    Are uploaded images searchable?

    Anyone with the unique link can view the image until it expires. dropimg.io does not provide a public image gallery, and temporary image pages are excluded from search indexing.

    Can I delete an image early?

    Yes. After upload, dropimg.io provides a private delete control so you can remove an image before it expires.

    What image formats are supported?

    dropimg.io supports PNG, JPEG, WebP, and GIF uploads up to 10 MB.

    Does dropimg remove photo metadata?

    Yes. dropimg.io strips EXIF and similar metadata (including GPS when present) from JPEG, PNG, and WebP uploads before storing the image.
