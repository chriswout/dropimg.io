Privacy

dropimg.io is a temporary image hosting utility. We designed it to store as little personal information as possible.

  • No accounts.
  • Original filenames are not stored.
  • EXIF and similar metadata (including GPS when present) are stripped from JPEG, PNG, and WebP uploads before storage.
  • Images expire and are deleted after 24 hours by default.
  • You can delete an image earlier with the private delete token.
  • We do not store raw IP addresses; rate-limit fingerprints are hashed daily.
  • Shared image pages are marked noindex.
  • There is no public gallery of uploads.

Deleting an image removes it from dropimg.io. Copies already downloaded by viewers cannot be recalled.
