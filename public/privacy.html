Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

dropimg.io (“dropimg.io,” “we,” “us”) is a temporary screenshot and image sharing utility. You paste, drop, or choose an image; we give you a short link that expires. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your choices.

By using dropimg.io you agree to this Policy and our Terms of Use.

1. What dropimg.io is (and is not)

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No public gallery or browseable library of uploads.
  • Share pages are marked noindex so they are not meant for search results.
  • Image links are temporary by design (24 hours by default).
  • Anyone who has the unique link can view the image until it expires or is deleted.

2. Information we process

2.1 Images you upload

When you upload an image we process and store:

  • The image file bytes (after metadata handling described below).
  • Technical metadata we need to serve it: MIME type, file size, optional width/height, creation time, expiry time, and a random short URL slug.
  • A hashed delete token (not the raw token) so you can delete the image early if you still have the delete control from upload.

We do not store the original filename from your device.

Supported formats: PNG, JPEG, WebP, and GIF, up to 10 MB. SVG and other formats are rejected.

2.2 Metadata stripping

For JPEG, PNG, and WebP we attempt to strip EXIF and similar embedded metadata (which can include GPS and camera details) before storage. If stripping fails, the upload is rejected rather than stored with that metadata intact. GIF files are stored as uploaded (GIF does not use EXIF in the same way).

Stripping reduces risk; it is not a guarantee that an image cannot reveal anything about you. Do not upload screenshots or photos you are not willing to share with anyone who obtains the link.

2.3 Network and abuse-prevention data

To rate-limit uploads and reduce abuse we derive a daily rotating hash from your IP address (using a server secret). We store that hash with the upload record for quota purposes. We do not store raw IP addresses in our application database for uploads.

Cloudflare (our hosting and security provider) may process IP addresses and request metadata as part of operating the network, CDN, DDoS protection, and logs according to Cloudflare’s own terms and privacy commitments.

2.4 Aggregate product analytics

We may record product events (for example: upload succeeded or failed, share page viewed, image deleted or expired) in Cloudflare Analytics Engine. These events are for operating and improving the service. They are designed around operational fields such as event type, MIME type, size, or reason codes—not account profiles.

2.5 Abuse reports

If you submit a report via /abuse or email, we process the information you provide (such as the image link/slug, reason, and optional details) so we can investigate and respond.

2.6 Data stored only on your device

The browser may keep local data such as recent upload links (including delete tokens) and language preference in localStorage. That data stays on your device unless you clear site data. We do not use it to build cross-device accounts.

2.7 Cookies

dropimg.io’s core upload and share flow does not require account cookies. Essential infrastructure or security tooling operated by Cloudflare may set cookies as needed to deliver and protect the site. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the marketing or share experience described here.

3. How we use information

  • To provide temporary image hosting and share pages.
  • To enforce size, format, rate, and quota limits.
  • To delete expired or user-deleted images.
  • To investigate abuse, security incidents, and legal requests.
  • To understand aggregate reliability and usage of the service.

4. How long we keep data

  • Images: automatically deleted about 24 hours after upload, or earlier if you use the delete control / delete link from the upload response.
  • Database records associated with an image are removed or marked deleted as part of that lifecycle; expired objects are cleaned up by our systems.
  • Abuse reports may be retained longer as needed to handle the report and protect the service.
  • Security and infrastructure logs held by Cloudflare follow Cloudflare’s retention practices.

Deleting an image from dropimg.io removes it from our storage. We cannot erase copies that viewers already downloaded, screenshotted, or re-uploaded elsewhere.

5. Who can see your images

Share URLs are randomly generated and not listed publicly, but they are not a login. Anyone with the link can open the image while it is live. Treat links like secrets: do not post them publicly if the content is sensitive.

6. Sharing with processors

We use infrastructure providers to run dropimg.io, including Cloudflare (Workers, DNS/CDN, object storage, database, analytics, and security). They process data only to provide those services to us.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to address abuse.

7. International processing

dropimg.io is delivered over Cloudflare’s global network. Your uploads and requests may be processed in data centers outside your country. If you use the service, you understand that transfer.

8. Children

dropimg.io is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Do not use the service to upload content that exploits or endangers minors. See our Terms of Use and abuse reporting.

9. Your choices

  • Do not upload an image you do not want shared via link.
  • Use the delete control soon after upload if you need early removal.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove local recent-drop history.
  • Contact us about a privacy question or deletion request related to reports or residual records we control.

10. Contact

Privacy and abuse: abuse@dropimg.io

11. Changes

We may update this Policy as the product changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
