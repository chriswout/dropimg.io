Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

DropIMG (“DropIMG,” “we,” “us”) is a temporary image sharing utility. You paste, drop, or choose an image; we give you a short link that expires. Optional accounts add sign-in, history, and DropIMG Pro. This Policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your choices.

By using DropIMG you agree to this Policy and our Terms of Use.

1. What DropIMG is (and is not)

  • An account is optional. Anonymous 24-hour uploads still work.
  • No public gallery or browseable library of uploads.
  • Share pages are marked noindex so they are not meant for search results.
  • Links are temporary. Free links expire in about 24 hours. Pro links expire after at most 30 days.
  • Anyone who has a working share link can view the image until it expires, is deleted, or (if protected) until they unlock it.

2. Optional accounts

If you sign in, we store the email address you provide so we can send one-time magic links and recognize your account. There is no account password.

3. Authentication

  • Sign-in uses a one-time magic link sent to your email.
  • The raw magic-link token is not stored. We keep a hash until the link is used or expires (about 15 minutes).
  • A signed-in session uses an HttpOnly session cookie. Only a hash of the session token is stored.
  • You can sign out of the current device or all devices from your account page.

4. Uploads

4.1 Images

When you upload an image we process and store:

  • The image file bytes (after metadata handling described below).
  • Technical metadata needed to serve it: MIME type, file size, optional width/height, creation time, expiry time, and a random short URL slug.
  • A hashed delete token so you can delete the image early if you still have the delete control.
  • If you are signed in, an association between the image and your account so it can appear in My drops.

We do not store the original filename from your device.

Supported formats: PNG, JPEG, WebP, and GIF. Anonymous and Free uploads are limited to 10 MB. Pro uploads may be up to 50 MB when that limit is enabled. SVG and other formats are rejected.

4.2 Retention

  • Anonymous and Free: about 24 hours, or sooner if you delete the image.
  • Pro: up to 7 or 30 days, depending on the expiry you choose. Even Pro links expire. There is no permanent storage.
  • Expired or deleted images are removed from active hosting. Cleanup is not always instantaneous; background jobs and storage lifecycle rules finish the removal.

4.3 Metadata stripping

For JPEG, PNG, and WebP we attempt to strip EXIF and similar embedded metadata before storage. If stripping fails, the upload is rejected. GIF files are stored as uploaded.

4.4 Password-protected links

Pro users can put a password on a share link. The password value is not stored in plaintext. We store a one-way hash used only to check later unlock attempts. Recipients who unlock a link get a short-lived cookie for that image only.

5. Security and abuse controls

To rate-limit uploads and reduce abuse we derive a hashed fingerprint from your IP address using a server secret. We store that hash for quota and abuse controls. We do not store raw IP addresses in our application database for uploads.

Cloudflare (our hosting and security provider) may process IP addresses and request metadata as part of operating the network, CDN, DDoS protection, and logs.

6. Integrations

You can create a personal integration token for the browser extension or ShareX. The raw token is shown once. After that, DropIMG keeps only a hash of the token. Treat the token like a password. Revoking it stops new uploads from that connection. Existing share links are not changed.

7. Billing

Paid DropIMG Pro checkout is handled by Paddle. Paddle receives the payment and billing information needed to charge you. DropIMG stores the subscription identifiers and status we need to provide Pro (for example whether Pro is active, when the current period ends, and whether cancellation is scheduled). We do not store your full card number.

8. Analytics

We record first-party product events in Cloudflare Analytics Engine (for example: a page view, an upload result, a sign-in request, a checkout step, or a dashboard action). Events use low-cardinality fields such as event name, plan, billing interval, upload surface, and reason codes. We do not put raw integration tokens, passwords, or email addresses in analytics.

9. Account deletion

When you delete your account we:

  • cancel an active Pro subscription first, if we can reach Paddle;
  • remove your active DropIMG images;
  • revoke connected integration tokens;
  • sign you out everywhere;
  • anonymize the local account record (the email is replaced with a tombstone address and the account is marked deleted).

If billing cancellation fails, the account is not deleted so we do not leave an unpaid or uncanceled subscription behind. Deleted-account cleanup is not always instantaneous for every stored object or log.

10. Browser extension and device data

The official Chrome / Edge extension can capture the visible tab or a region you draw. Anonymous captures use the same upload rules as the website. If you connect an account, the extension stores your personal token only on that device (not in synced storage). Recent links and language/theme preferences may also stay in local browser storage until you clear them.

11. Cookies

Signed-in use needs a session cookie. A language cookie remembers your locale choice. Unlocking a protected image sets a short-lived cookie for that image. Cloudflare may set essential security cookies. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the product described here.

12. How we use information

  • To provide temporary image hosting, share pages, and optional accounts.
  • To send magic-link emails and keep you signed in.
  • To enforce size, format, rate, quota, and plan limits.
  • To provide Pro features and talk to Paddle about your subscription.
  • To delete expired, user-deleted, or moderated images.
  • To investigate abuse, security incidents, and legal requests.
  • To understand aggregate reliability and usage of the service.

13. Who can see your images

Share URLs are randomly generated and not listed publicly, but they are not a login. Anyone with the link can open an unprotected image while it is live. A password only helps if you keep that password and the link private.

14. Processors

We use infrastructure providers to run DropIMG, including Cloudflare (Workers, DNS/CDN, object storage, database, email sending, analytics, and security) and Paddle for paid subscriptions. They process data to provide those services to us.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to address abuse.

15. International processing

DropIMG is delivered over Cloudflare’s global network. Uploads and requests may be processed in data centers outside your country.

16. Children

DropIMG is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). Do not upload content that exploits or endangers minors.

17. Your choices

  • Use DropIMG without an account.
  • Delete an image from the success screen or My drops while you still can.
  • Revoke integration tokens and sign out of all devices.
  • Cancel Pro from Manage billing (access continues until the period ends if you cancel at period end).
  • Delete your account from the account page.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove local recent-drop history.

18. Contact

19. Changes

We may update this Policy as the product changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
