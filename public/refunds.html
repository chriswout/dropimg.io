Refund Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This policy covers DropIMG Pro, the only paid product on dropimg.io. It sits alongside our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

1. There is a free plan, so you can try before you pay

DropIMG works without an account and without paying. Pro adds longer link lifetimes, larger uploads, password-protected links, full history, and integrations. Nothing about the free plan expires or degrades if you decide not to subscribe, so we expect most people to know whether Pro is worth it before they buy.

2. Cancel any time

You can cancel from Manage billing on your account, or from the Link order page linked in your receipt. There is no cancellation fee and no minimum term.

When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and then stops. You are not charged again.

3. Refunds for the period already paid

We do not refund the remainder of a period you have already paid for, and we do not pro-rate part months or part years. You keep Pro for the time you bought.

Two exceptions, both of which we will honour:

  • Your statutory rights, including the EU and UK 14-day right of withdrawal described below.
  • Our mistake. If you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, charged for a plan you did not choose, or Pro was unavailable for a meaningful part of your billing period because something on our side broke, write to us and we will refund it.

4. EU and UK right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU or the UK, you have 14 days from the start of your subscription to withdraw from it and receive a refund, without giving a reason.

Because DropIMG Pro is a digital service supplied immediately, you are asked to agree at checkout that supply begins straight away. Where you have used the service during those 14 days, the amount refunded may be reduced in proportion to what you used, as the law allows. In practice, for a subscription at this price, we refund in full.

5. Who processes the payment

Checkout, invoicing, and tax are handled by Stripe, which is the merchant of record for every DropIMG Pro purchase. Stripe runs this under its Link brand, so your purchase is recorded as sold through Link and your receipts and invoices arrive from Link rather than from us.

For the same reason, the charge appears on your statement as LINK.COM* DROPIMG and not as dropimg.io. If you are scanning a statement for an unfamiliar charge, that is us. Refunds are issued back to the original payment method and show as a reversal from Link.

Stripe's and Link's own buyer terms also apply to your purchase. Where they give you more than this policy does, they win.

6. How to ask for a refund

Email support@dropimg.io from the address on the account, and tell us what you would like refunded and why. Please include the date of the charge. You do not need an order number, but it helps if you have the receipt Link sent you.

You can also ask Link support directly, from the order page in your receipt. Either route reaches the same place, and Link can settle most requests within 60 days of the original charge without waiting on us.

We aim to answer within a few working days. Approved refunds are typically back on your card within 5 to 10 working days once Stripe has processed them, depending on your bank.

7. Chargebacks

Please write to us before disputing a charge with your bank. A chargeback costs us a fee and takes far longer to resolve than an email. If a chargeback is raised, we may suspend Pro on the account while it is being investigated.

8. What is not refundable

  • Time already elapsed on a subscription period, except as set out above.
  • Charges on an account that was suspended or terminated for breaking the Terms of Use.
  • Loss caused by images expiring. Every link on DropIMG is temporary by design, on both Free and Pro, and this is stated before you upload.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy by posting a revised version with a new "Last updated" date. The version in force when you were charged is the one that applies to that charge.

10. Contact

Billing, refunds, and account questions: support@dropimg.io. See our contact page for everything else.
