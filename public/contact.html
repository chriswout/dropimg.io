Contact

Last updated: August 30, 2026

DropIMG is a small temporary image-sharing service. There is no phone line and no live chat — email reaches us and we read all of it.

Billing, refunds, and accounts

support@dropimg.io

Use this for anything about DropIMG Pro: a charge you do not recognise, cancelling, a refund request under our Refund Policy, an invoice or receipt, sign-in trouble, or deleting your account and its data.

Write from the email address on the account where you can, and include the date of the charge if it is a billing question. Payments are handled by Stripe as merchant of record, under its Link brand, so your receipt comes from Link and the charge reads LINK.COM* DROPIMG on your statement.

Abuse, copyright, and illegal content

abuse@dropimg.io — or use the report form, which is faster.

Include the share link or slug so we can find the image. Links on DropIMG expire on their own, often within hours, so report promptly: once an image is gone we can no longer act on it.

For copyright, choose the copyright option on the report form and give us enough detail to identify both the material and your claim.

Privacy and data requests

support@dropimg.io, with "privacy" in the subject. What we store and for how long is set out in the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account and everything attached to it yourself from your account page.

What to expect

We aim to answer within a few working days. Abuse reports are looked at sooner. We do not send marketing email and will only reply to what you asked about.
