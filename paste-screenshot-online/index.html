    Why clipboard paste beats “save then upload”

    Support chats, Discord, Slack, and bug reports are full of “one quick screenshot.” Saving to Downloads, finding the file, and attaching it is friction. Pasting from the clipboard keeps the loop in one browser tab.

    dropimg.io returns a temporary URL that lasts 1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days — your choice. Send it wherever the conversation is happening. You have a screenshot in your clipboard and need a URL — paste it here.

    Prefer one-click from the browser?

    Our Chrome / Edge extension captures the visible tab and uploads it for you — same temporary links, no account. See /browser-extension.

    Formats

    PNG and JPEG screenshots work best. WebP and GIF are fine too. Max 10 MB. SVG is not accepted.
